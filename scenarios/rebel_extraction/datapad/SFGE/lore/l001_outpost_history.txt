TITLE: Outpost Origin Report
CLASS: DECLASSIFIED // ALLIANCE INTELLIGENCE
DATE: 3 ABY
---
This outpost was originally constructed as a
moisture farming relay station during the
late Republic era. After the fall of the
Jedi Order, the Empire converted it into a
regional communications hub.

Local resistance cells have operated in the
area for approximately two standard years.
The outpost's remote location and aging
Imperial garrison make it an ideal staging
point for intelligence operations.

Note: Multiple hidden access points have been
identified throughout the facility. Refer to
sector maps for current entry coordinates.
---
END REPORT
