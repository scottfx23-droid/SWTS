OPERATIVE DEBRIEF - SHADOW EXTRACTION

The classified materials secured during this
operation contain Imperial weapons development
data of critical strategic importance.

Analysis indicates the Empire is constructing
a new class of capital ship at an orbital
shipyard in the Kuat system. Preliminary
specifications suggest a vessel significantly
larger than an Imperial Star Destroyer.

Alliance leadership has authorized further
reconnaissance. All operatives with Shadow
Clearance are to standby for follow-up
deployment orders.

This information is restricted to operatives
with SHADOW CLEARANCE designation. Unauthorized
disclosure is punishable under Alliance
military code Section 7, Article 3.

May the Force be with us all.

-- General Cracken, Alliance Intelligence
